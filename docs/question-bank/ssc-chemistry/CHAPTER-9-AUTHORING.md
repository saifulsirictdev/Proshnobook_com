## Scientific and safety conventions

- NumericalpHuses dilute concentration model,25°C,base10log. H⁺(aq)is shorthand for hydrated proton; pH7neutral is tied to25°C. Neutrality generally means[H₃O⁺]=[OH⁻]. Litmus is a coarse classroom test; no exactpHclaimed fromlitmus. Indicator acid datasets avoid the marginalpH5transition region.
- Strong does not mean concentrated/safe; weak does not mean noncorrosive. H₂SO₄is commonly classed strong but its second proton is not assumed fully dissociated under all conditions.
- Full neutralization stoichiometry is explicit. Atomic masses:H1,C12,N14,O16,S32,Na23,K39,Ca40,Cl35.5. All10mass problems supply their needed formula-mass relationships.
- Salt hydrolysis depends on ions. FeCl₃source answer gives the simplified school equation but qualifies real hydratedFe³⁺stepwise hydrolysis; it does not assert all Fe(OH)₃must precipitate.
- Extended boiling's magnesium-bicarbonate net reaction can giveMg(OH)₂+2CO₂. Temporary/permanent are removal categories, not “permanent means impossible to remove.” Precipitates require separation.
- BODis biochemical oxygen demand under stated testconditions,CODchemical oxygen demand. CODusually≥BODforcomparable valid tests,not always strictlygreater. Neither is identical to current dissolvedoxygen or complete safety certification.
- No medical dosing, soil amendment dose, household acid/base gas-production steps, arsenic-filter construction recipe or drinking-water chemical dosing. No tasting unknown reagents or mixingbleach/acids. Source gasdiagram is theoretical. Textbook antacid and bodilypHquestions are curricular facts, not personalized medical advice. Safety explanations prohibit substitutingNaOHforantacid or neutralizing skin exposure with a second chemical.
