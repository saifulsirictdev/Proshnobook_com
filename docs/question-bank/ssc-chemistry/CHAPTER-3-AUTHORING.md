- Nuclide A and charge values are selected counting inputs, not claims of naturally abundant or stable species. Some singly charged teaching species would be unstable/unusual; no preparation/handling instruction is given. Proposed A+1 isotopes are conditional comparisons, not stability assertions.
- Isotope X data are **hypothetical**, not natural isotope tables. Mass numbers are used as approximate u-masses explicitly. Fraction arithmetic keeps means exact under these assumptions. Inverse abundance assumes only two isotopes. The 50:50 variant correctly permits an arithmetic mean; the other variants require weighting. Expected counts in 10000 atoms are expectations, not guaranteed actual sample counts.
- H–Ca configurations are neutral ground states. A shell’s observed occupation is not its maximum 2n² capacity. Cr/Cu are covered separately in conceptual questions; the H–Ca shortcut is not extended to all elements.

## Scientific corrections rather than copying source errors

- Z=9 identifies **F**, not Cl. Cl has Z=17.
- Only protium among the three discussed H isotopes has no neutron; the statement is not generalized to all hydrogen.
- Electron mass is very small but not zero.
- Rutherford’s 1911 nuclear model did not discover the neutron and does not fully explain atomic stability or line spectra.
- Bohr’s model is historical, mainly useful for H/one-electron species; modern orbitals are probability descriptions, not literal circular or elliptical trajectories. Sommerfeld’s historical elliptical paths are not modern orbitals.
- One orbital holds at most 2 electrons; s/p/d/f subshells contain 1/3/5/7 orbitals and hold 2/6/10/14. Subshell and orbital are not interchangeable. The source’s n=5 naming typo is not repeated.
- Ordinary neutral Aufbau filling places 4s before 3d, but this does not establish a fixed energy/removal order for every ion. Cr `[Ar]3d5 4s1`, Cu `[Ar]3d10 4s1` are retained.
- Approximate six-hour half-life belongs to **Tc-99m**, not ground-state Tc-99. Medical use is expert-controlled; no dose or treatment advice.
- Approved food irradiation does not automatically make food radioactive and is not a substitute for all food-safety controls.
- The textbook’s illustrative power-plant picture is not evidence of current Rooppur capacity or operating status; no such current factual claim is made.
