## Scientific and safety handling

- Textbook MCQ2 and SQ3 recommending lime on a bee sting are recorded as excluded, not silently rewritten and not counted. Clinical sting treatment is not taught as a simple acid–base neutralization rule here.
- Antacid questions explain chemistry only, with no personal medicine/dose recommendation. Concentrated acid, combustion, barium salts, reactive metals and gas examples are theoretical classroom questions, not instructions for unsupervised experiments.
- Oxidation numbers are formal bookkeeping, not an assertion of literal atomic ionic charges in all covalent molecules. N₂O's queried N value is explicitly an **average**. H₂O₂ and metal hydride exceptions are identified in literal concept questions.
- `2FeCl₂ + Cl₂ → 2FeCl₃` has **two** reduced Cl atoms from Cl₂; the four spectator chloride atoms are not counted as reduced atoms. Tests count atoms in the active reactant, not all Cl on the left.
- Heat signs reverse with reaction direction; heat amounts scale with the specified stoichiometric extent and unchanged physical states. Rate values are average gas-volume rates at fixed measurement temperature/pressure, not concentration-normalized kinetic constants. Display precision is up to four decimals.
- In equilibrium questions, pressure is explicitly changed by volume change at fixed temperature; no blanket inference for arbitrary inert-gas additions. For CaCO₃/CaO equilibrium both solid phases remain present. Catalyst changes rate, not equilibrium composition at fixed temperature.
