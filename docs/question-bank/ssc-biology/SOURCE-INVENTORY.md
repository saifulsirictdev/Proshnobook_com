## Editorial safeguards and limits

Bone is living connective tissue and its matrix has complementary mineral stiffness and collagen tensile properties. Remodeling is normal; persistent imbalance can weaken bone. Articular cartilage lacks perichondrium; avascular cartilage relies on diffusion. Hair/nails are keratinized integumentary structures, not extra bones. The 206 adult count is conventional and not identical at every age/person. Regional skeleton drawing deliberately is not a bone-count inventory.

Joint does not always mean freely mobile; not all skull joints are immobile and vertebral-body joints are not fixed. Tendon is connective tissue transmitting muscle pull; ligament links bones, many are collagen-rich. Pectoral/pelvic girdles and axial support distinguished. Muscle contraction can generate force without shortening or during active lengthening; simple antagonism is useful but not every real action. Skeletal voluntary classification includes reflex/postural control. ATP required for cross-bridge cycling and Ca2+ transport; actin/myosin lengths do not themselves shrink. Torque uses perpendicular moment arm; models explicitly omit specified other torques.

Osteoporosis is not solely calcium deficiency or a women-only disease; absence of pain does not certify bone strength. RA is not rheumatic fever, gout or OA; different disorders may coexist. OA affects multiple joint tissues. No single RF or BMD result guarantees every diagnosis/future event. No universal supplement dose, unprotected prolonged sunlight, borrowed medication or self-stopped glucocorticoid. Hot joint with fever, injury deformity, numbness or cold limb need prompt/urgent qualified assessment, not forced reduction or continued play. Prepared data/paper models avoid invasive tests, patient blame and privacy violations.
