### Model lengths — editorial practice targets, not invented official rules

- Paragraphs: **143–156 words**, targeting **about 150**.
- Compositions: **229–249 words**, targeting **about 250**.
- Correspondence: **126–142 words**, including address/subject/salutation/closing where applicable; no mandatory syllabus word limit is asserted.

The SSC blueprint defines marks but no universal word count for these three items. The 150/250 targets are editorial choices for these prepared exercises. Different original source word requirements and original marks remain fully visible; they have not silently become the practice limit or practice mark.

## Content limitations and review decisions

- These are **possible models**, not unique correct strings, official keys or compulsory memorisation texts. Teachers should accept other coherent and appropriate writing.
- Paragraphs remain one paragraph; compositions develop the subject across four paragraphs. They are not outlines padded into alleged full answers.
- Personal experiences, correspondence names/dates and additional details are illustrative fiction, not claims about the assistant's life or verified real events. No message is sent to any recipient.
- Email models use reserved `example.com` addresses. There are no real account credentials, phone numbers, sending actions or production integrations in the writing data.
- A school book-fair application is dated **10 February**, before its requested **21 February** event, rather than mechanically using a later date.
- Models avoid unsupported numerical statistics, fatalistic claims about poverty and disability, harmful gender stereotypes and guaranteed health outcomes. Health-related topics emphasise qualified advice and safe general prevention rather than unverified cures.
- Opinions, narrative choices, wording and factual details still need substantive teacher review. Automated tests validate structure, provenance and completeness; they do not confer content approval.
