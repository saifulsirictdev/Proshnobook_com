## ssc-p2-letter-email-001 — Setting up a School Canteen

Original: Dhaka · Board 2024 · physical PDF page 2 · Q13 · 10 original marks · SSC English 2nd Paper_2018_2024.pdf

Source-backed practice. Writing focus: Setting up a School Canteen. Suppose, you are Shuvo/Sova, a student of class IX. Your school is a reputed school but there is no canteen in your school. Now, write an application to the Headmaster of your school for setting up a canteen in your school. Use imaginary contact details where none are specified.

Current practice: 10 marks · subtype application · model 134 words

### Possible model answer

```text
20 November 2026
The Headteacher
Our School
Subject: Application for a school canteen
Sir/Madam,
I am Shuvo, a student of Class IX. On behalf of the students, I would like to request a canteen on our school campus. We remain at school for several hours, but there is no suitable place to obtain hygienic refreshments during the break. Some students must go outside, which takes time and can create safety concerns.
A modest canteen providing affordable food and safe drinking water would be helpful. We suggest regular hygiene checks, clearly displayed prices and proper waste bins. The arrangements should also consider students with different dietary needs.
We would be grateful if you considered the proposal and discussed a practical plan with the school committee.
Yours faithfully,
Shuvo
Class IX
On behalf of the students
```

Please ask the librarian about the available edition instead of buying a book simply because I recommended it. Different readers enjoy different things. If you borrow it, I would love to hear which story interests you most.
Your friend,
Rafi
