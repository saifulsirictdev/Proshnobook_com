## ssc-p2-paragraph-001 — The Life of a Farmer

Original: Dhaka · Board 2024 · physical PDF page 2 · Q14 · 10 original marks · SSC English 2nd Paper_2018_2024.pdf

Source-backed adapted practice. Write a paragraph of about 150 words on "The Life of a Farmer".

Current practice: 10 marks · target about 150 words · model 143 words

### Possible model answer

A farmer grows the food on which our everyday lives depend. In Bangladesh, farmers cultivate rice, vegetables, fruit and many other crops. Their work begins with preparing the soil and choosing suitable seeds. They must then water the fields, control pests responsibly and wait for the harvest. Rain, drought, floods and changes in market prices can make their income uncertain. Not all farmers own the land they cultivate, and some struggle to meet production costs. Better storage, fair access to markets, reliable advice and affordable finance can improve their position. Consumers can help by valuing agricultural work and reducing food waste. Farming also requires knowledge, planning and careful observation of nature. A farmer is therefore not merely a producer of crops but an important contributor to the nation's food security and economy. We should respect this work and support safer, more sustainable agriculture.

Environmental pollution occurs when harmful substances or excessive noise damage our surroundings. Smoke from vehicles and factories can reduce air quality, while untreated waste can pollute rivers and canals. Plastic litter blocks drains and harms animals. These problems affect health, livelihoods and the natural systems on which people depend. Their causes are often connected with careless consumption, weak waste management and poor enforcement of reasonable standards. Protecting the environment is not a task for one special day. ## ssc-p2-paragraph-012 — Tree Plantation

Original: Mymensingh · Board 2023 · physical PDF page 36 · Q12 · 15 original marks · SSC English 2nd Paper_2018_2024.pdf

Source-backed adapted practice. Write a paragraph of about 150 words on "Tree Plantation".

Current practice: 10 marks · target about 150 words · model 151 words

### Possible model answer

Tree plantation means planting and caring for trees in suitable places. Trees provide shade, support wildlife and help protect soil. They also absorb carbon dioxide as they grow. Planting a sapling, however, is only the beginning of the task. Its survival depends on the species, the location, the season and continued care. A tree that is unsuitable for a narrow pavement or an electric line can create problems later. Our school can consult knowledgeable local people before planning a plantation programme.
