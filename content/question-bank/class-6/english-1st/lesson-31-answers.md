# Lesson-31: Too Much or Too Little Water

Class 6 · English 1st Paper · Draft · Teacher review pending

Source: NCTB English for Today, revised for 2026. Printed pages [90, 91]; PDF pages [95, 97]. Use the cited textbook sections. Not a full model test; marks unset.


## Teacher answers

Accept equivalent accurate wording and supported interpretations.

### short-answers

1. River water can overflow its banks and cover large areas of land.

2. Land, towns and villages can be flooded.

3. Rivers may dry up and underground water levels may fall, creating shortages.

4. People may have difficulty finding enough safe water for drinking and daily needs. This is a supported application of the lesson’s discussion.

5. Both excess water in floods and a shortage of water can cause suffering.

### vocabulary

1. flow over an edge or limit

2. the sides of a river

3. very large

4. below the surface of the ground

5. not having enough
