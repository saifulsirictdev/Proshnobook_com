# Lesson-32: An Invitation for Robin

Class 6 · English 1st Paper · Draft · Teacher review pending

## Teacher answers

Accept equivalent accurate wording and supported interpretations.

### short-answers

1. He has received an invitation from his aunt to stay with her family in the countryside during his holiday.

2. He is Robin’s cousin and is about Robin’s age.

3. It asks him to let them know when he will arrive.

4. They say they will meet him at the bus station.

5. Its main purpose is to thank his aunt for inviting him and for the enjoyable stay.

### vocabulary

1. a request to visit or take part

2. meet or welcome someone in this context

3. tell us; inform us

4. an unfamiliar range of experiences

5. feel someone’s absence and wish to be with them

### letter

Dear Rafi,
I would like to invite you to our home next Friday afternoon. We can read our new storybooks and work on our class poster together. Please ask your parents first. Our families can agree on the time and travel arrangements. Let me know whether you can come. I hope we can spend an enjoyable afternoon together.
Your friend,
Nabil
