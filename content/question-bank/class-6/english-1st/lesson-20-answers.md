# Lesson-20: Hason Raja: The Mystic Bard of Bangladesh

Class 6 · English 1st Paper · Draft · Teacher review pending

Source: NCTB English for Today, revised for 2026. Printed pages [61, 62]; PDF pages [67, 66]. Use the cited textbook sections. Not a full model test; marks unset.


## Teacher answers

Accept equivalent accurate wording and supported interpretations.

### short-answers

1. A bard is someone who writes poems and songs.

2. He was born into a wealthy landlord family in Sylhet in 1854.

3. He came to feel that material possessions were meaningless and began thinking deeply about the creator, life, death and humankind.

4. It says Hason Udash was published in 1906 and contained 206 songs.

5. The passage says he called himself “Pagla Hason Raja”, translated there as “Crazy Hason Raja”.

### vocabulary

1. great material comfort or expensive living

2. things owned by someone

3. a book forming a collection or part of a work

4. expressed or showed

5. farther than; outside a stated limit
