## Passage P1: Learning in the School Garden

Every Wednesday, Class Six spends twenty minutes caring for a small garden behind the school library. Their teacher, Ms Rahman, helped them make a plan, but the pupils chose the plants. They planted spinach, beans and marigolds in three separate beds. A narrow path lets everyone walk between the beds without stepping on young plants.

At first, the children carried water in open buckets. Much of it spilled on the path. Rupa suggested using old bottles with small holes in their caps. The bottles helped the pupils water the roots slowly. The teacher checked that the bottles had no sharp edges before anyone used them.

The class keeps a notebook beside the library window. Each group records the weather, the plants' growth and the work it has done. When some leaves turned yellow, the pupils did not immediately add more water. They asked their teacher for help and compared their notes.

After the first harvest, the class shared the vegetables with the school kitchen. The children were proud, not because their garden was large, but because they had learned to work together and use resources carefully.
