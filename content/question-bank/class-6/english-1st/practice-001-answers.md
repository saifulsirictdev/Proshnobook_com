## story

modelAnswer: Mina recognised the badge of a nearby school. She did not open the bag or take it home. Instead, she went to the school office and told the teacher on duty where she had found it. The teacher kept the bag safely and contacted the class teachers. Soon a worried pupil arrived with his mother. Before handing it over, the teacher asked them to describe the bag and its contents. Their description matched. The boy thanked Mina because his exercise books were inside. Mina was glad that she had asked a responsible adult for help. She returned home and told her family what had happened.

assessment: [
  "Continues the given opening without contradiction",
  "Has connected events and a plausible ending",
  "Uses consistent narrative tense and clear references",
  "Avoids encouraging unsafe contact with strangers"
]

note: Original model completion. Other coherent, safe endings are acceptable; this is not a memorisation-only answer.


## paragraph

modelAnswer: Working together at school helps us learn from one another. Last month, our class organised a small reading corner. We first discussed what we needed and listened to different suggestions. Some pupils collected suitable books, while others cleaned the shelf with help from a teacher. Another group made labels so that readers could find stories easily. We divided the work according to our abilities, not according to who was the loudest. When two friends disagreed about the labels, they explained their ideas and chose a clear design together. We also made a simple rule about returning books after reading. The corner was small, but everyone had a chance to contribute. I learned that teamwork requires patience, fairness and responsibility. A shared task becomes easier when we respect others and ask for help when necessary.

assessment: [
  "Addresses a shared school task with relevant details",
  "Explains responsibilities and listening",
  "Presents connected ideas as a paragraph",
  "Uses age-appropriate vocabulary, grammar and punctuation"
]

note: No official word count or marks assigned until guidance is verified. Personal examples may differ.
