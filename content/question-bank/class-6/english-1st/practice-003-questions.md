## Passage P1: Listening to the Rain

During the rainy season, the pupils of Hillview School noticed that water often collected near the main gate. Some children hurried across it, while others took a longer dry path. One morning, their teacher, Ms Das, asked the class to observe the area from a safe place inside the school grounds. She reminded them not to walk into moving water or approach an open drain.

The class began a five-day observation project. At the same time each afternoon, a group noted whether it was raining and whether water was visible near the gate. They drew a simple table instead of trying to measure the water themselves. On the third day, they saw that fallen leaves covered part of a nearby drain.

The pupils gave their notes to the headteacher. She contacted the maintenance team, who cleared the leaves using suitable equipment. The children watched from a distance; they did not handle the waste. On the last day, less water was visible after a shower. However, the teacher said that one observation did not prove that the problem was completely solved. The school would continue checking the area.

The project showed the pupils how careful records, safe behaviour and help from responsible adults could improve a shared place.
