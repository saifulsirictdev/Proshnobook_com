### dialogue

Nila: What is your favourite food?

Omar: I like vegetable khichuri best.

Nila: Where do you usually have it?

Omar: I usually have it at home with my family.

Nila: Who prepares it?

Omar: My father usually prepares it, and I help set the table.

Nila: How does it taste?

Omar: It tastes delicious. We wash our hands before eating.
