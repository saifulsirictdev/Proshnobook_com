## Teacher answers

Accept equivalent accurate wording and supported interpretations.

### mcq

1. A. There was noise around them

The introductory situation explicitly mentions noise at the book fair.

2. B. England

James gives England as his place of origin.

3. C. London

The officer distinguishes origin (Australia) from the place of immediate departure (London).

4. D. His father was transferred there

Sajed explains that his father’s transfer is why he is there.

5. A. An architect

Mamun names her profession before describing her current project.

6. B. A school building

The present project is a school on the outskirts of Dhaka.

7. C. She works part-time at the Town Hall Library

Mary identifies the mother’s work; this does not prove the reason for the crying.

### short-answers

1. He can apologise and say, “I’m sorry, I couldn’t catch your name.”

2. Australia identifies where he comes from; London is where he is coming from on this journey.

3. Joy says that he is sure Sajed will like Khulna and the school, and tells him that they are already friends.

4. She usually designs offices and shopping complexes. At the moment she is designing a school building.

5. No. Rabeya suggests he may be hungry, while Mary suggests he may be crying for his mother. Both are guesses.

### vocabulary

1. hear and understand

2. moved to another work location

3. a person who designs buildings

4. outer parts of a town or city

5. occupation; type of work

### language-use

1. “What do you do?” asks about a profession or usual work in this context. “What are you doing?” asks about the activity at the moment or around that time.

2. He could ask, “Where are you from, Sajed?”

### dialogue

Rafi: Mina, this is my friend Karim.

Mina: Hello. I’m sorry, I couldn’t hear your name because of the noise.

Karim: My name is Karim. Nice to meet you.

Mina: Nice to meet you too. Where are you from?

Karim: I’m from Rajshahi. How about you?

Mina: I’m from Sylhet. Are you travelling today?

Karim: Yes, I’m waiting here with my family.

Rafi: Let’s move away from the crowd so we can talk more easily.
