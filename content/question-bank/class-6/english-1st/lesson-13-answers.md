### short-answers

1. It celebrates Bangladesh’s victory in the Liberation War and honours the people who sacrificed their lives.

2. It is the National Martyrs’ Memorial.

3. They hoist the national flag and take part in commemorative cultural or educational programmes. Offering flowers is another example.

4. It is dedicated to soldiers and civilians who lost their lives during the war.

5. It means people from many different backgrounds and occupations come together to show respect.

### vocabulary

1. respect or honour shown publicly

2. remember and honour through a special observance

3. showing love for one’s country

4. an arrangement of flowers, often circular, used as a tribute

5. encouragement; something that motivates

### paragraph

In this imagined account, our school observes Victory Day with a respectful programme. We gather in the morning and learn why the day matters. Our teacher explains the importance of remembering those who sacrificed their lives during the Liberation War. Some pupils read poems, while others sing patriotic songs. We prepare a display about freedom, responsibility and respect for people. I help arrange the books on the display table. At the end, we discuss how honesty, kindness and learning can help us serve our country. The programme leaves me feeling grateful and thoughtful.
