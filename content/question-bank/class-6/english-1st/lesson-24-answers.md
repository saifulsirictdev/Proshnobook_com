## Teacher answers

Accept equivalent accurate wording and supported interpretations.

### short-answers

1. The UAE and Oman are examples. Saudi Arabia, Qatar and Bahrain are also listed.

2. It encourages language skills, including English, Arabic or other languages used where they will work.

3. Better work and fairer pay can help workers support their families and contribute to the economy.

4. He works in a hospital in Cairo, Egypt.

5. He suggests the Cairo Museum and the Pyramids. Port Suez is also mentioned.

### vocabulary

1. in or to another country

2. be entitled to something because it is right or fair

3. advantages or employment-related support

4. give or play a part in something

5. connected with established customs
