### poem

1. Gwendolyn Brooks is credited.

2. The speaker has to choose a mother and a father.

3. There seem to be many different possible parents to choose from.

4. The poem mentions short and tall, thin and stout people.

5. The realisation comes just before the speaker wakes.

6. The speaker chooses the same parents they have always had.

7. It surprises the speaker and makes them glad.

8. The poem expresses the speaker’s affection and appreciation for their own parents through the imagined choice in a dream.

### vocabulary

1. choose

2. rather large or solid in body build

3. woke suddenly

4. felt uncertain or thought about something

5. pleased; happy
