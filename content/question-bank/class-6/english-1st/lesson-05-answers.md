This belief is explicit; it does not mean all jobs have identical tasks.

### short-answers

1. He goes from door to door, takes the rubbish from the bins and puts it in his van.

2. He returned to Sankar and collected everything from the bins.

3. When he was absent, rubbish piled up and the area became unhygienic. This shows that his regular work helps keep the neighbourhood clean.

4. He believes the whole area would turn into a large dump because rubbish would remain uncollected.

5. I could say, “Thank you for helping to keep our neighbourhood clean. Your work is important.”

### paragraph

A cleaner helps to keep the lane near our home tidy. She starts work early and sweeps the common spaces carefully. She collects waste using suitable tools and places it in the collection area. Her work makes the lane more pleasant for everyone. If rubbish were left there, it could pile up and create unhealthy conditions. We should not make her work harder by throwing litter on the ground. I can help by using the correct bins and keeping sharp waste safely separated for responsible adults to manage. I respect her work and thank her for her service.
