### short-answers

1. Hygiene means the rules and practices of keeping good health.

2. Suitable food and nutrition, physical exercise, adequate rest and sleep, and cleanliness are four examples. The passage also mentions proper medical care.

3. It means food containing carbohydrates, fat, protein, vitamins, minerals and water in appropriate proportions.

4. Wash hands before eating, bathe regularly and wear clean clothes, and brush teeth regularly. Other accurate practices listed in the passage are acceptable.

5. He usually exercises every day, jogging for half an hour in the morning and playing table tennis in the afternoon almost regularly. He sometimes plays football and does yoga once a week.

### vocabulary

1. worry; uneasiness

2. nourishment from food; the process of obtaining and using nutrients

3. health-maintaining practices, including cleanliness

4. the relative amount of each component

5. at repeated or usual intervals

### paragraph

Healthy habits support our body and mind. We should eat a varied, balanced diet, drink safe water and keep our food clean. Washing hands before meals and keeping our clothes and surroundings clean are useful habits. Regular activity, suitable rest and enough sleep are important too. When I feel unwell, I tell a trusted adult so that I can get appropriate care. I do not take medicine on my own. Different people have different health needs, so a qualified health professional can guide us when necessary. These habits support health, but being ill is never a reason to blame or shame someone.
