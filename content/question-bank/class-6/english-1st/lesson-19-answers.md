5. They help a person notice visual details and sounds rather than rushing past relevant information.

### vocabulary

1. a principle or instruction guiding what to do

2. details or signs that help explain or discover something

3. go from one side to the other

### paragraph

Before I begin a task, I pause and read the instructions carefully. I look for details that show what I need to do. I listen to the teacher and ask when something is unclear. If I am solving a problem, I collect clues instead of guessing immediately. I check my work before handing it in. These habits help me work thoughtfully and notice mistakes.
