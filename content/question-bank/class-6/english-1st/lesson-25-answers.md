## Teacher answers

Accept equivalent accurate wording and supported interpretations.

### short-answers

1. She is watching George Harrison playing guitar and singing.

2. He helped by organising the Concert for Bangladesh and raising awareness and support, not by coming to fight in the war.

3. It was held on 1 August 1971 at Madison Square Garden in New York.

4. It raised awareness of suffering during the Liberation War and raised support and money for relief through UNICEF.

5. It asks listeners to understand people’s suffering and come forward to help save lives.

### vocabulary

1. a person concerned with human welfare and dignity

2. people watching or listening to a performance

3. help for people suffering in a crisis

4. after a person’s death

5. great suffering or anxiety
