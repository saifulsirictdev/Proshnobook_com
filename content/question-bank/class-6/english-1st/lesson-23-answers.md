## Teacher answers

Accept equivalent accurate wording and supported interpretations.

### short-answers

1. His uncle Reza wrote the letter.

2. Congo is in Central Africa.

3. He takes care of children’s education in a community school as a member of a voluntary organisation.

4. He was born in Indonesia, moved to Singapore at eight and later completed schooling in Denmark before university studies in the United States.

5. He has lived, studied and worked in different countries and values understanding people’s languages, cultures and aspirations. His outlook connects him with humanity beyond one country.

### vocabulary

1. keep up; preserve

2. done by choice rather than compulsion

3. hopes or ambitions

4. worldwide; international

5. serious disagreement or fighting
