# Lesson-29: Taking a Test

Class 6 · English 1st Paper · Draft · Teacher review pending

Source: NCTB English for Today, revised for 2026. Printed pages [86, 87]; PDF pages [93, 94]. Use the cited textbook sections. Not a full model test; marks unset.


## Teacher answers

Accept equivalent accurate wording and supported interpretations.

### short-answers

1. They are twin sisters in class six.

2. They are taking an English test during the half-yearly examination.

3. Careful reading helps them understand the instructions and answer the actual questions.

4. Do not copy from someone else’s script.

5. They should use the waste bin rather than throw litter in public places.

### vocabulary

1. directions explaining what to do

2. a pupil’s written exam answer paper in this context

3. a person who receives payment

4. small pieces of rubbish left in an unsuitable place

5. with attention and caution
