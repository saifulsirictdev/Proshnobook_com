### short-answers

1. They are planning how to spend a week’s vacation.

2. It attracts attention to a two-day trip, the beach and sunrise.

3. He suggests the Chimbuk Mountains in Bandarban because he has wanted to climb a mountain.

4. He wants to see wild animals and take pictures of the forests and the sea.

5. No. They agree to consider the options before deciding.

### vocabulary

1. a period away from usual school or work

2. possible choices

3. an idea offered for consideration

4. very attractive or impressive in this dialogue

5. choose after considering possibilities

### dialogue

Mina: What should we make for our class project?

Rafi: How about a poster on keeping the school clean?

Mina: That sounds useful. We could include drawings and short instructions.

Rafi: Let’s ask our group members for their ideas before deciding.

Mina: Good idea. Then we can agree on the work together.
