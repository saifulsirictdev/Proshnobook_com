### short-answers

1. Bread, cucumber, tomato and cheese are four examples. Butter or mayonnaise, cooked egg and lettuce are also listed.

2. Place the other slice of bread on top.

3. They help readers follow the steps in the intended order.

4. It says to be extra careful with a sharp knife. For pupil practice, a responsible adult should handle sharp tools and supervise preparation.

5. Chopping cuts food into pieces, while stirring mixes it by moving a spoon or similar utensil around.

### vocabulary

1. the foods or substances used to make a dish

2. apply in a thin layer over a surface

3. put in a chosen order or position

4. make a liquid flow from one container to another

5. cut into thin flat pieces

### paragraph

Before our classroom snack activity, we wash our hands and clean the table. A responsible adult checks the ingredients, food allergies and storage conditions. We use clean utensils and wash the vegetables. The adult handles sharp knives and any cooking that is needed. We read the instructions and arrange the prepared ingredients in order. We do not taste raw egg or touch a hot pan. After the activity, we put food away safely and tidy the workspace. Following a sequence helps us learn English while adult supervision helps keep the activity safe.
