### poem

4. What does the poem suggest about trunks and tails? [Printed 19 / PDF 24]

5. Why is the title playful rather than a literal description of human hands? [Printed 19 / PDF 24]

6. What effect does repeating the idea of holding tails have? [Printed 19 / PDF 24]

8. What does “It never fails” suggest in the poem’s playful description? [Printed 19 / PDF 24]
