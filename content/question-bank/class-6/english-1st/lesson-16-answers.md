### poem

1. Christina Georgina Rossetti is credited.

2. Boats sail on rivers and ships sail on seas.

3. The clouds sailing across the sky are described as prettier.

4. It refers to a rainbow arching across the sky.

5. Its arch appears to bridge the sky, like a beautiful bridge higher than the trees.

6. It links their movement across the sky with the movement of boats and ships, making the comparison vivid.

7. The poem celebrates natural beauty by presenting clouds and the rainbow as even prettier than boats, ships and river bridges.

8. No. The road from earth to sky is an imaginative description of the rainbow, not a construction project.

### vocabulary

1. move smoothly across the sky

2. rainbow; a curved arch of colour

3. rises above; extends higher than

4. the sky

5. spans; forms an arch across

### sentences

The poem first mentions boats and ships, then finds the clouds prettier. It next mentions river bridges and compares them with a rainbow. These comparisons express the speaker’s admiration for natural beauty.
