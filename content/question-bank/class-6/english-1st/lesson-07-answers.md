# Lesson-07: Holding Hands

## Teacher answers

Accept equivalent accurate wording and supported interpretations.

### poem

1. The textbook credits Leonore M Link.

2. They are described as holding hands by holding one another’s tails.

3. They walk along trails and in circus rings.

4. It calls them handy things, showing that they are useful for keeping the elephants linked while walking.

5. Elephants do not hold human hands in the scene. The poet compares their trunk-and-tail connection with people holding hands.

6. The repetition emphasises how the elephants stay linked and gives the short poem a memorable rhythm.

7. One main idea is the connection of elephants moving together. The repeated image of holding tails presents them as a linked group, like people holding hands.

8. It suggests that the elephants keep doing this when they walk. This is the poem’s description, not scientific proof about every elephant.

### vocabulary

1. paths; tracks

2. useful; convenient

3. the elephant’s long nose

4. the main woody stem of a tree; a large storage chest

5. a piece of jewellery worn on a finger; the sound made by a telephone

### sentences

The elephant lifted its trunk. A thick trunk supports the tree. My aunt wears a silver ring. I heard the ring of the telephone.
