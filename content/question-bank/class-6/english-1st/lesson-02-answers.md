## Teacher answers and guidance

Accept equivalent accurate answers. Writing models are illustrative, not memorisation requirements.


### mcq

1. A. Dhaka

The introductory situation places her school in Dhaka.

2. B. An A in her English test

Lily reports her English test grade.

3. C. Folk songs

The introduction describes his love of folk songs.

4. D. His new album has been a hit

His news concerns the success of an album of Lalon’s songs.

5. A. An inter-school essay competition

The occasion was Independence Day and the event was an essay competition.

6. B. He had the flu

Sujan says he had flu and spent most of the time in bed.

7. C. He dropped it somewhere

He is uncertain and says he thinks he dropped it somewhere.


### short-answers

1. They live in a small town in Tangail district.

2. He praises her and says that he is proud of her.

3. The album contains Lalon’s songs.

4. He is in class six and reports winning the first prize in the essay competition.

5. His father had given him the pen, and it was very dear to him.

6. She failed the test.

7. Shanu says that her parents will understand and that she will do better next time.


### vocabulary

1. people of approximately the same age

2. a collection of recorded songs or music

3. a popular success; a very successful album in this context

4. precious; valued; beloved

5. wonderful; excellent

Teacher note: Hit means a popular success here, not a physical blow; dear expresses personal value, not necessarily high price. Album may have other meanings in other contexts.


### responses

1. Congratulations! Well done!

2. I’m sorry about the result. I hope your next match goes better.

3. Thank you! That is very kind of you.

4. I’m sorry to hear that. How are you feeling now?

5. Congratulations! I’m very happy for you.

- Responds appropriately to good news, bad news or thanks

- Uses polite, supportive language

- Does not blame the person or promise an unverified outcome


### dialogue

Rina: Hello, Sami. You look happy. Do you have some good news?

Sami: Yes! I have been selected for our school music programme.

Rina: Congratulations! You have worked hard for this.

Sami: Thank you. Your encouragement means a lot to me.

Rina: When will the programme take place?

Sami: It will be next Thursday after our lessons.

Rina: Are you feeling ready to perform?

Sami: I am a little nervous, so I am practising every afternoon.

Rina: I hope it goes well. I look forward to hearing you sing.

Sami: Thanks, Rina. I am glad you will be there.

- Relevant news and congratulation

- Acknowledgement and logical turn-taking

- Supportive language without invented textbook claims

- Clear speaker labels and comprehensible sentences

Teacher note: Original writing model: Rina and Sami are fictional practice characters, not characters from the source dialogue. No official word limit or turn count is asserted.
