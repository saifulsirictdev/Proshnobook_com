# Lesson-33: The Garden

Source: NCTB English for Today, revised for 2026. Printed pages [94, 95, 96, 97, 98, 99, 100]; PDF pages [99, 100, 102, 101, 103, 105, 104]. Use the cited textbook sections. Not a full model test; marks unset.


## Teacher answers

Accept equivalent accurate wording and supported interpretations.

### short-answers

1. The textbook credits Arnold Lobel.

2. He admires Frog’s garden and wishes he had one too.

3. He gives Toad flower seeds to plant.

4. Toad expects the seeds to obey immediately, but seeds do not respond to shouted commands. The story gives them imagined feelings for humour.

5. He advises leaving the seeds alone for a few days and letting sun and rain reach them.

6. He reads a story and sings songs. He also reads poems and plays music.

7. He sees little green plants growing out of the ground.

8. A reader can see the value of patience: Toad cannot make seeds grow immediately by worrying or shouting.

### vocabulary

1. plant structures from which new plants can grow

2. speak or cry very loudly

3. without much light

4. marks indicating direct speech or quoted words

5. a punctuation mark expressing strong feeling or surprise
