## 11. Answers (10 marks)

Title: The Bag at the School Gate

Rafi stopped beside the bag. He did not know who owned it. He called the teacher who was standing near the gate. Together, they took the bag to the school office. The teacher checked for a name while Rafi waited. A label inside named a pupil from another class. The office contacted that pupil’s class teacher. Soon a worried child arrived and described the bag. The teachers checked that it was really hers before returning it. She thanked Rafi for reporting what he had found. Rafi felt pleased that he had asked for help instead of guessing.

## 12. Answers (10 marks)

My first day at my new school began with mixed feelings. I felt excited about meeting classmates, but I was also a little nervous. My mother took me to the school gate and wished me well. Inside, a teacher showed me the way to my classroom. A pupil smiled and offered me a seat beside her. During the break, she introduced me to two friends. They explained where we kept our books and how to borrow a library book. I asked questions whenever I was unsure. By the end of the day, the building no longer felt strange. I went home feeling welcome and hopeful. That day taught me how small acts of kindness can help a newcomer feel comfortable.

## 13. Answers (10 marks)

Pupil: Excuse me, may I ask about this basket?

Guide: Of course. What would you like to know?

Pupil: The label describes its use, but I cannot find a date. When was it made?

Guide: I do not have a confirmed date at the moment.

Pupil: Should I write an approximate year in my notebook?

Pupil: Is there another source we could check?

Guide: I can check the museum records and ask the curator.
