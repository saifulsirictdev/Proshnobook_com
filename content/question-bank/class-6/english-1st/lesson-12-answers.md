### short-answers

1. It has attractive natural scenery but is less known to many people. The title suggests an overlooked destination, not a place nobody has ever seen.

2. The ceramic hill and the Someshwari River are two examples; the nearby hills and forests are also described.

3. It says that the river changes its colour as the seasons change.

4. The passage places Shusong Durgapur about 170 kilometres north of Dhaka.

5. It preserves and promotes the cultures and traditions of the indigenous groups described in the passage.

2. far away; distant

3. very clear; transparent

4. gentle flow

5. ways of travelling from one place to another

### paragraph

I would like to visit Birishiri after reading about its scenery. The textbook describes a ceramic hill, rivers and views towards distant hills. I would enjoy looking at the landscape and taking photographs from safe public places. Before travelling, my family would check the current route, weather and local guidance. We would respect residents and their cultural traditions. We would carry our litter back and avoid disturbing plants or entering unsafe abandoned sites. I have not visited the place yet, but learning about it has made me curious about the natural beauty of Bangladesh.
