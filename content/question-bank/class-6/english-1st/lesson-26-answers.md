# Lesson-26: Buying Clothes

Source: NCTB English for Today, revised for 2026. Printed pages [78, 79, 80, 81, 82]; PDF pages [83, 85, 86, 87, 84]. Use the cited textbook sections. Not a full model test; marks unset.


## Teacher answers

Accept equivalent accurate wording and supported interpretations.

### short-answers

1. He asks for one without a chain.

2. It is too big for him.

3. He chooses a small, sky-blue polo shirt.

4. The assistant says the trial room is at the back.

5. The stated price is 300 taka, and the shirt is on sale that week in the story.

### vocabulary

1. loose-fitting

2. without a decorative pattern or writing in this context

3. long narrow bands of contrasting colour

4. a pattern of squares

5. be the right size and shape for someone
