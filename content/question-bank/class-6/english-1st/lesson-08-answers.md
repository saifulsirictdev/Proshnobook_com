## Teacher answers

Accept equivalent accurate wording and supported interpretations.

### mcq

1. A. Guests will visit that night

The opening paragraph gives the reason for the shopping.

2. B. Imran often forgets necessary items

The text explicitly mentions his forgetfulness.

3. C. Three

This is existing stock, not the dozen more he proposes buying.

4. D. He checks the list

The stage direction says that he looks at the list.

5. A. A paper or jute bag

Both paper and jute are accepted in his request.

6. B. 535 taka

535 is the bill; 550 is the amount paid.

7. C. 15 taka

The stated change is consistent with 550 minus 535.

### short-answers

1. Section A requests half a kilogram of potatoes; Section B requests two kilograms. Section B also adds salt, which Section A did not request.

2. He asks for one litre of milk and one kilogram of sugar.

3. He requests nine kinds: beans, tomatoes, potatoes, cauliflowers, eggs, salt, oil, sugar and milk.

4. No explicit reason is stated. A reader may suggest reducing plastic-bag use, but this is an inference, not a stated motive.

5. The salesperson says that they do not use plastic bags in that shop.

### dialogue

Customer: Good morning. May I have two notebooks, please?

Shopkeeper: Certainly. They are thirty taka each.

Customer: That makes sixty taka altogether. Here is one hundred taka.

Shopkeeper: Thank you. Here are your notebooks and forty taka change.

Customer: Thank you. I have brought my own bag.

Shopkeeper: You’re welcome. Have a good day.
