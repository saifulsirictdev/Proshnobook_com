## Passage P1: A Visit to the Local Museum

On Thursday, thirty pupils from Riverbank School visited a small local museum. Before leaving, their teacher, Mr Sen, divided them into five groups. Each group received a different question to investigate. His purpose was to help everyone look closely instead of simply copying labels.

At the entrance, a guide explained two rules: visitors must not touch the objects, and they should speak quietly. Sami was interested in an old wooden boat. He first thought it was a toy, but the label said that fishers had used it on a nearby river. The guide explained that the river had once been an important route for travel and trade.

In another room, the pupils saw a display of woven baskets. There was no date on one label. Rather than guessing, Liza wrote, "Date not given," in her notebook and prepared a question for the guide. The guide promised to check the museum records; she did not provide a date immediately.

Back at school, each group presented one finding and one unanswered question. The class learned that a good visitor notices details, respects shared objects and admits when information is missing.
