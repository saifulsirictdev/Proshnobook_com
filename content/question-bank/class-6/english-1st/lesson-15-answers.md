### short-answers

1. Her usual activities no longer feel sufficiently fulfilling, and she wants to help people who need care and companionship.

2. She talks about her school, friends, studies and family, and listens to the stories they tell her.

3. No. It describes Suraiya’s plan, visits to understand people’s needs and her request for help.

4. The recipient is a friend who used to be her colleague.

5. Suraiya wants to provide a supportive, family-like place for older people and asks a friend to work together. The letter does not describe a commercial business.

### vocabulary

1. no longer doing one’s former paid job

2. a person one works with professionally

3. very unhappy

4. help; assistance

5. with keen interest or anticipation

### paragraph

Pupils can offer friendship to older people without assuming that everyone has the same needs. With a trusted adult’s guidance, we can ask whether someone would enjoy a visit or a conversation. We can listen carefully to their stories and speak respectfully. If they would like help reading a book or writing a message, we can assist with permission. We should respect privacy and never share personal information without consent. Medical and personal-care needs should be handled by responsible adults and qualified carers. A small act of kindness can create a welcome connection between generations.
