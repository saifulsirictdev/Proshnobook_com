### poem

1. The title is “The Chaos”.

2. Gerard Nolst Trenité is credited.

3. It illustrates that similar spellings can have different pronunciations and that spelling alone does not reliably predict every sound.

4. They let readers notice and practise pronunciation differences rather than only being told that English is difficult.

5. It creates a playful, humorous tone by presenting a crowded series of tricky words and joking about the speaker’s struggles.

6. The speaker says that he learned to speak when young but still had difficulty writing much later. This is part of the poem’s humorous voice, not a verified biography.

7. The poem gives groups with similar spellings but different sounds, showing why pronunciation must be checked and practised.

8. They practise the diphthong /aɪ/, as in five.

### vocabulary

### sentences
