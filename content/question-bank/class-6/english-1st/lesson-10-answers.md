### mcq

1. A. Herbs and plants

This describes the source’s terminology, not proof of treatment effectiveness.

2. B. No; it describes their use in earlier ages

The paragraph discusses ancient civilisations and practices passed down through generations.

3. C. Ancient Egypt

Egypt is one of the examples named, alongside India, China, Greece and Rome.

4. D. A pharmacist

The dialogue labels identify the speakers.

5. A. The lotion

He chooses the lotion and asks its price. This is a reading fact, not a product recommendation.

### short-answers

1. It says that people used herbal medicines in earlier ages and in several ancient civilisations.

2. The paragraph lists India, China, Greece, Egypt and Rome.

3. It means that people have passed those practices from one generation to the next.

4. No. The age or natural origin of a practice alone does not establish safety or effectiveness. A qualified health professional should guide treatment decisions.

5. He asks how much it costs.

### vocabulary

1. a way of thinking or feeling about something

2. belonging to the distant past

3. successive groups of people at different periods in a family or society

4. formally recommend or order a medicine or treatment as a qualified practitioner

5. a qualified professional who prepares or supplies medicines and advises on their safe use

### dialogue

Child: I do not feel well. Could you help me?

Adult: Of course. Tell me what you are feeling.

Child: I have a headache, and I feel tired.

Adult: Thank you for telling me. We will arrange appropriate advice and care.

Child: Should I take something from the medicine cupboard?

Adult: Do not take medicine on your own. Let us ask a qualified health professional.

Child: All right. I will stay with you and explain if I feel worse.

Adult: Yes. You do not have to manage this alone.
